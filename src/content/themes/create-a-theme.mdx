---
title: Create your own theme
description: Build a theme from your brand colors in tweakcn, for light and dark mode.
---

After this guide, you will have a theme that uses your brand colors in both
modes. Use this guide when you have brand colors but no theme. To start from a
screenshot or a description, see [Generate a theme with AI](/ai/generate).

## Before you begin

- Your brand colors as hex values: one primary color and, if you have them,
  secondary and accent colors.
- Your brand fonts, if they are on Google Fonts.

<Steps>
<Step title="Start from a neutral preset">

Open the theme picker and select **Default** or **Clean Slate**. A neutral
preset gives you good base, border, and muted colors. You change only the brand
colors.

</Step>
<Step title="Set the brand colors in light mode">

On the **Colors** tab, set these colors:

| Group | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Primary | Your main brand color. Buttons and links. |
| Primary | Primary foreground | Text on primary. Use white or near-black. |
| Accent | Accent | Hover and selected backgrounds. A light tint of the brand color. |
| Base | Background | The page background. |
| Base | Foreground | The main text color. |
| Border & Input | Border, Ring | Borders and the focus ring. |

Press Tab after each value.

</Step>
<Step title="Set chart colors">

Set **Chart 1** to **Chart 5**. Use colors that are easy to tell apart. Start
with your primary color.

</Step>
<Step title="Set the dark mode colors">

Switch to dark mode. Set the same colors again. Dark mode usually needs:

- A dark background and a light foreground.
- A primary color with a little more lightness than in light mode.
- Borders with low contrast against the background.

</Step>
<Step title="Set the fonts and radius">

On **Typography**, select your fonts. On **Other**, set **Radius**. Use `0` for
sharp corners and `0.75rem` or more for round corners.

</Step>
<Step title="Check the contrast">

Open **⋮** > **Contrast**. Fix each failed pair in both modes.

</Step>
<Step title="Save the theme">

Select **Save** and give the theme a name. See
[Save and share a theme](/sharing/save-and-share).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Copy colors from other themes

To keep one part of another theme, open that theme, copy the values from
**Code**, then [import](/themes/import-css) them into your theme. The import
merges the values with your current theme.
