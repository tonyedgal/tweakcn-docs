---
title: Preview a theme on your site
description: Show the theme from the tweakcn editor on your own running app, before you install it.
---

The **Custom** preview tab loads your own app and applies the editor theme to
it live. Use it to check a theme on your real pages.

<Frame caption="The Custom tab before you enter a URL.">
![Custom preview tab with setup steps](/images/live-preview.png)
</Frame>

## Before you begin

- An app that uses shadcn/ui CSS variables on `:root`.
- The app running at `http://localhost:3000`.

<Steps>
<Step title="Add the preview script">

Add this script inside `<head>` of your app.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Next.js (App)">

```tsx app/layout.tsx
<head>
  <script src="https://tweakcn.com/live-preview.min.js" />
</head>
```

</Tab>
<Tab title="Vite">

```html index.html
<head>
  <script src="https://tweakcn.com/live-preview.min.js"></script>
</head>
```

</Tab>
</Tabs>

The Custom tab also has snippets for Next.js (Pages) and Remix.

</Step>
<Step title="Enter your URL">

In the editor, select the **Custom** preview tab. Enter
`http://localhost:3000` in the URL field.

</Step>
<Step title="Edit the theme">

Change a color. Your app in the preview changes at once.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Note type="warning" title="Development only">
Add the script only in development. Remove it before you deploy.
</Note>

<Note type="info" title="Not tested in these docs">
These steps come from the editor and the tweakcn source. Test them with your
app before you depend on them.
</Note>
